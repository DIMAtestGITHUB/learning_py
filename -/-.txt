Song Analysis: “Stressed Out” by Twenty One Pilots
	Introduction

The song “Stressed Out” by Twenty One Pilots strongly connects to the topic of social media and teen anxiety. The song explores feelings of pressure, stress, and the loss of simplicity in modern life. Many of these emotions reflect how teenagers feel in today’s digital world, especially when influenced by social media expectations and online comparison.

	Analysis of Key Lyrics

One of the most important lines in the song is:

“Wish we could turn back time, to the good old days.”

This lyric reflects a desire to escape pressure and responsibility. Teenagers today often feel overwhelmed by expectations, including those created by social media. Platforms constantly push teens to present a perfect image, which increases anxiety and stress.

Another meaningful lyric is:

“Wake up, you need to make money.”

This line represents societal pressure and unrealistic expectations placed on young people. Social media often promotes success, popularity, and wealth, making teens feel as though they must achieve these standards quickly. This pressure can negatively affect mental health.

	Connection to Social Media Anxiety

The overall tone of the song is anxious and nostalgic, which mirrors the emotional state of many teens affected by social media. The song’s themes of pressure, insecurity, and stress directly connect to the research from Part 1, which shows that comparison culture and constant online presence increase anxiety levels among adolescents.

Social media removes the sense of privacy and relaxation that teens once had. Similar to the song’s message, teens today often feel that they cannot escape expectations, whether offline or online.

	Conclusion

In conclusion, “Stressed Out” effectively represents the emotional impact of social pressure on teenagers. The song’s lyrics, tone, and message strongly connect to the issue of social media–related anxiety. It highlights how modern teens struggle to balance expectations and mental well-being in an increasingly digital world.
